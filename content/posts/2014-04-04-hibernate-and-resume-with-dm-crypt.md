I could conclude there is no way out-of-the-box with ubuntu and I now use truecrypt.

Don't make the mistake and try hibernate/resume with [dm-crypt](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dm-crypt) with big 2TB disk. This trying costs me three full days copying data with no result :(. I repeated it successfully with truecrypt.
 
I already had [truecrypt](http://www.truecrypt.org) running for years ... for some sensitive private data. I'd like to try something new and failed.
